## About
Northwind Smile Dental is a modern family dental clinic in Madhapur, Hyderabad. Friendly team of 3 dentists. Open since 2018.

- **Address:** 2nd Floor, Plot 14, Madhapur Main Road, Hyderabad 500081 (fictional)
- **Phone (AI receptionist):** see website
- **Languages spoken:** English, Hindi, Telugu

## Hours
- Monday–Saturday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sunday: closed
- Appointments are 30 minutes. Last appointment starts at 5:30 PM.

## Services and prices

| Service | Duration | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Check-up & cleaning | 30 min | ₹800 |
| Teeth whitening | 30 min (consult) / 60 min (session) | from ₹6,000 |
| Emergency visit (pain, broken tooth, swelling) | 30 min | ₹1,000 |
| Invisalign / clear aligner consultation | 30 min | Free |

Prices are starting prices. The dentist confirms the final cost after examination.

## Payment & insurance
- We accept UPI, cards and cash.
- We accept cashless insurance from major providers. Bring your insurance card. For reimbursement, we give a detailed bill.
- EMI available for aligners and whitening above ₹10,000.

## Booking rules
- Book by chat, by phone, or walk in (walk-ins wait for a free slot).
- To book we need: full name, mobile number, service, preferred date and time. Email is optional.
- Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before the appointment.
- Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit.

## Emergencies
- If someone has severe pain, swelling, bleeding that won't stop, or a knocked-out tooth: **do not give medical advice**. Ask them to call the clinic number right away for the earliest emergency slot. If it is a medical emergency (difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, trauma), tell them to go to the nearest hospital or call 108.

## FAQs
1. **Do you treat children?** Yes, from age 3.
2. **Is parking available?** Yes, free two-wheeler and car parking in the building basement.
3. **How long does whitening last?** Usually 1–2 years, depending on food and habits.
4. **Does cleaning hurt?** Most people feel no pain; sensitive teeth may feel mild discomfort.
5. **How often should I get a check-up?** Every 6 months.
6. **Do you do root canals?** Yes, after a check-up the dentist will advise. Book a check-up first.
7. **Can I get a same-day appointment?** Often yes, if slots are free. Ask to check availability.
8. **Is the Invisalign consultation really free?** Yes, including a 3D scan.
9. **What should I bring?** Any previous X-rays or prescriptions, and your insurance card.
10. **Are you open on public holidays?** Closed on national holidays; open on other festival days with reduced hours (check by phone).

## What the assistant must NOT do
- No diagnosis, medicine names or dosage advice.
- No promises about final treatment cost.
- No booking outside opening hours or on Sunday.
